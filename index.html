Research Logs

Understanding Social Norms for Continuous Location Tracking Apps
May 2026 — Present
Research Intern · IIT Kharagpur · under Prof. Mainack Mandal
  • Developing a framework to extract six Contextual Integrity parameters — Attribute, Subject, Sender, Recipient, Purpose and Appropriateness — from large-scale app reviews using LLM-based information extraction.
  • Designed a client–server architecture that collects and processes 4M+ Google Play reviews, with automated CI extraction using Llama, Gemma, Qwen and Mistral.
BERTScore > 0.90 4M+ reviews 4 LLMs evaluated
Pre-operative Prediction of Ovarian Malignancy using ML
May — July 2026
Research Intern · IIT Bhubaneswar · under Prof. Debi Prosad Dogra
  • Built an end-to-end, leakage-aware ML pipeline to differentiate benign vs. malignant ovarian masses before surgery on a 112-patient AIIMS Bhubaneswar dataset with 184 attributes.
  • Consensus feature set from five selection methods (MI, ANOVA, Lasso, RF importance, RFE); evaluated five classifiers under Leave-One-Out CV.
  • Outperformed the FDA-cleared ROMA score and an OVERA-style reconstructed panel; SHAP confirmed reliance on established clinical drivers.
96.6% accuracy AUC ~0.99 beats FDA benchmarks
Copy–Move Forgery Detection using U-Net Segmentation
Jun 2025 — Jan 2026
Research Intern · Jadavpur University · under Prof. Ram Sarkar
  • Detected and localised copy–move forgeries by training segmentation models to predict pixel-level binary masks.
  • Implemented EfficientNet, ResNet, MultiResUNet and FPN architectures across diverse loss functions to find the most robust configuration.
Dice > 0.80 4 datasets 4 architectures
Breast Cancer Recurrence Prediction using Wrapper Selection

Research Intern · Heritage Institute of Technology · under Prof. Arpita Talukdar
